Changelog
#########

The first production LimeRFE was revision v1.0 and so this changelog starts with changes from that point.

.. note::
   Details of internal-only versions which were never released are not included here.

v1.31
****

Updates from LimeRFE v1.0 to LimeRFE v1.31

MCU 
===

GPIO pins have been modified so that GPIO0 and GPIO1 now support Arduino interrupt routines (IR).

LNA_EN and PA_ENn have been moved at the MCU from pins 1 and 32 to pins 14 and 13, respectively.

GPIO1_MCU and GPIO0_MCU are now connected to MCU interrupt pins INT1 and INT0 (pins 1 and 32), respectively.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_MCUGPIOs.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 1. MCU GPIO changes

To support TDD operation without an interrupt routine (IR), U167 has been added.
   
An inverter was also added to make BAND38_TX_ENn active low.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_TDDINV.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 2. New TDD control inverter

R138 has been added so that the ICSP is directly connected to +5V_MCU node.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_ICSPbyp.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 3. ICSP bypass

RFFE
====

Additional SAW filters with matching networks have been added:

* Cell Band 01: U160
* Cell Band 02: U164, L121
* Cell Band 03: U165, L122, L123
* Cell Band 07: U166, L124, L125

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_SWfilters.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 4. New band filters

Since the PE4245 was not available on the market, it has been replaced with the HMC544AE.

Affected components: U61, U63.

Control signals for the new switches are generated by U137. These control signals are now 3 V instead of the previous 2.5 V.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_RFSW.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 5. New RF Switches

The HAM3500 PA designated as MAAM-009560 has been replaced with the MMZ38333, and the surrounding circuitry has been modified accordingly.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_PA.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 6. New PA

The section between U57 (cellular predriver) and U86 (RF switch) has been changed from:

* MADL-011021-14150T (U160, limiter) 
* PAT1220-C-3DB-T5 (U161, attenuator)

to:

* PAT1220-C-3DB-T5 (U149, not fitted, attenuator)
* MADL-011008-14120T (U150, limiter)
* PAT1220-C-5DB-T5 (U161, not fitted, attenuator)
* Additionally, C150, C151, and L120 have been added.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_FRTXCTRLs.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 7. RF TX power controls

RF switch U86:

CELL band 7 connected RF4 changed to RF2

CELL band 38 connected to RF2 changed to RF6

Additionally, U99 swapped CELL band 7 and CELL band 38 on its pins RF2 and RF4

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_B7andB38.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 8. Cellular bands 7 and 38 connections

RF filter CELL band 38 in TX added

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_B38fil.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 9. New cellular bands 38 filter

Logical XOR circuit added for TDD contrlol for CELL band 38

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_B38CTL.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 10. B38 Control

Miscellaneous
=============

GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO4 and GPIO5 have been changed, which caused additional GPIO pins remapping on several connectors and components.

GPIO remapping on Q3–Q6, U27, U28, SW2, J13, R42–R45  and U23–U26:

from GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3

to GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_GPIOsremap.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 11. Remapped GPIOs 

This also remapped RELAY_0, RELAY_1, RELAY_2, and RELAY_3

from GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3

to GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_GPIOsREL.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 12. Remapped relay GPIOs 

J22 connector remapping:

GPIO5, GPIO3, GPIO3_CONN changed to GPIO1, GPIO5, GPIO5_CONN.

J12 connector remapping:

GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3_CONN, GPIO4, GPIO5 changed to GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5_CONN.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE_v1.31_changes_GPIOCON.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 13. Remapped GPIOs connector (J12)
